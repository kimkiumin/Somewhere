## Product thesis

Somewhere is a mobile-only hidden-destination experience. A dyad commits to one bounded,
qualified destination without seeing its identity, follows minimal directional guidance, and
reveals the place at arrival or whenever safety requires. It is not a ranking list, turn-by-turn
map, relationship claim, or proof that any one component caused an observed bundle-level result.
